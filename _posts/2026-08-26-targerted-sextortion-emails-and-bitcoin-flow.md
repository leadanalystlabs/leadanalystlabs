In this Scam Criminals obtain leaked/stolen Username & Password Combos from the dark web, hacker forums, etc. Then they send spear phishing emails to claiming they have hacked their systems and have X-rated recordings and/or pictures of the victims. The criminals demand payment via Bitcoin and claim they will release the video and send it to the victim's family, friends, work, etc.

As "Proof" they mention that the have the victims password(s) and they list one or more of the passwords. The emails come from temporary accounts and the source IP addresses are obfuscated/spoofed to avoid tracing.

Some instructions and a Bitcoin wallet address is included to send the payment to. This is what we can trace. 

If you get a couple of these wallet addresses you can use some Open source Intelligence(OSINT) techniques to do some digging.
